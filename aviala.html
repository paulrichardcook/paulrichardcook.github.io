fugl.io Flight Patterns: a powerful bird index


     

"Fugl" (/fuːl/, [fuːˀl]), the Norwegian word for bird. fugl.io Flight Patterns is a comprehensive database of bird descriptors, behavioural traits, range and sightings maps, and additional information. It gathers together nearly every type of information imaginable about birds into one place. Flight Patterns serves as both a field guide and a powerful research tool, with a people-friendly interface and engaging tools.

Founders

Ninni Victoria and Paul Richard met in 2024 and bonded instantly over a love of books and birds. What began as a hobby of collecting vintage field guides led to greater involvement in the birding community, evolving into volunteering and conservation work, and sparking the creation of fugl.io.

Ninni Victoria (she/her), born in Oslo, is a the Technology Projects Architect at BirdLife Norway, and is a board member of its Østfold chapter. She has a background in web/app development. Her favourite bird is the Roseate Spoonbill, and her nemesis bird is the Eurasian Pygmy Owl.

Paul Richard (he/him), originally from Canada, served as an analyst with the NHL's Nashville Predators hockey team in the United States for 10 years, utilizing statistics in player valuation and arbitration, before retreating to a quieter life. His nemesis bird was the White-Throated Dipper (Norway's national bird) until finally coming across one in November 2025, while his favourite is the Bearded Vulture.
