fugl.io Flight Patterns db


     

"Fugl" is the Norwegian word for bird. This is a comprehensive database of bird descriptors, behavioural traits, range, and additional information. We've gathered together every type of information imaginable about birds into one place. Colours, migration patterns, nest types... everything. Right now the .com domain is priced at $1,699. Alternatively, it could be .io, or flightpatternsdb.com.
